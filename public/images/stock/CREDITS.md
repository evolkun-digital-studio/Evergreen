## Content checks

- No faces are shown. The plastic-flakes photo shows only a hand and a sleeve. The stamp photo shows only hands and was cropped to remove text on a T-shirt.
- No company logos or branded vehicles are legible at the sizes used. Small manufacturer marks on the
  old phones and the forklift ("Hyster") are not readable at display size. Re-check them if a photo is
  ever shown larger.
- `industrial-scrap-yard.jpg` was taken at a shipbreaking yard in Jakarta. Ships are visible in the
  distance, and the crop favours the scrap in the foreground.
- `industry-hospital-corridor.jpg` is a low-light image. With the site treatment applied, it reads as near-monochrome.
